Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 12, 2026  |  App: Address Print Maker

Thank you for choosing Address Print Maker. We are committed to protecting your personal information and respecting your privacy. This policy outlines how information is gathered, managed, and safeguarded when using our application.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on your usage of Address Print Maker, we may collect the following details:

  • Account Information: When you register or sign in, your email address and profile identifiers are securely managed via Google Firebase Authentication.
  • User Content (Addresses & Labels): Contact names, postal addresses, labels, and customer details that you enter, import, or scan to generate print-ready PDFs and export lists.
  • App Diagnostics & Crash Reports: Anonymous technical logs collected through Firebase Crashlytics to monitor application health and address crashes.

2. Device Permissions Requested

Address Print Maker only requests permissions strictly necessary to perform its core label generation and scanning features:

  • Camera (OCR Text Scanner): Used strictly to scan text and addresses from parcels or physical documents using on-device Optical Character Recognition. Camera feeds are never uploaded or stored remotely.
  • Microphone / Speech Recognition: Used exclusively to facilitate hands-free address dictation. Voice data is processed in real time and is not recorded or retained.
  • Storage & File Access: Required to export created address PDFs, save generated labels, and import address books from Excel/CSV spreadsheets.

3. Third-Party Services

To deliver reliable cloud synchronization and app stability, we utilize Google Firebase services:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Firebase Crashlytics)

4. Data Storage & Security

All data submitted within the app is transmitted via encrypted HTTPS channels. Remote data is protected by Google Cloud infrastructure with strict security rules, ensuring only you have access to your saved address entries.

5. User Rights & Account Deletion

You have full ownership of your data. You may delete individual addresses or request full deletion of your account and related records directly through the application settings or by contacting our support team. Upon receiving your deletion request, your account data will be permanently wiped from our databases within 30 days.

6. Updates to This Policy

We may periodically update our Privacy Policy to reflect app updates and legal compliance. Changes will be posted directly to this web page.
