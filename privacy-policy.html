Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 12, 2026

App Name: Address Print Maker

Thank you for using Address Print Maker. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: If you sign in, we collect your email address and profile details through Firebase Authentication.
  • User Content (Addresses & Documents): Address data, contact names, labels, and notes that you enter or scan to generate PDFs, print, or export.
  • Crash and Performance Logs: Anonymous diagnostic information and crash logs gathered via Firebase Crashlytics to improve app stability.

2. Device Permissions Requested

  • Camera: Used strictly for OCR / Optical Character Recognition (scanning addresses directly from physical parcels or documents). Camera feeds are processed on-device and never stored on remote servers without your action.
  • Microphone / Speech Recognition: Used to enable voice-to-text input when dictating addresses. Audio data is processed in real time and is not stored.
  • Storage / Files: Used to import or export CSV/Excel files and save generated PDF address labels to your device.

3. Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party services that may collect information used to identify your device:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Crashlytics)

4. Data Storage & Security

Your address data and settings are stored securely using Google Cloud Firestore and locally on your device. We use industry-standard encryption protocols to protect your personal information.

5. Data Deletion & Account Removal

You have the right to delete your account and all associated address records at any time directly through the app settings, or by emailing us at your contact email below. Upon request, all data linked to your account will be permanently deleted from our servers within 30 days.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. You are advised to review this page occasionally for any updates.
